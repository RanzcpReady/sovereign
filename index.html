Sovereign

Crown Rule — a political city builder for iPhone

They will keep the roads. They will not keep you.

Build a place that works: roads across bare grass, housing, shops, heavy works downwind. Then the town starts having opinions. Five blocs want different things and none of them can all be satisfied, elections come whether you are ready or not, and if you never share power the court will eventually decide the town has outgrown being ruled.

No ads, no timers, no purchases. Sovereign makes no network requests at all. It works on a plane, in a tunnel, anywhere.
