Support

Sovereign: Crown Rule, for iPhone, iPad and Android

If something is broken, unclear, or simply wrong, write to sovereign.game@icloud.com. It is read by the person who made the game.

When reporting a problem it helps to include your phone model and its iOS or Android version, and what you were doing when it happened. If the game is still running, the ledger under the ☰ button has an export option that produces a copy of your save as text; attaching that makes most problems much faster to find.

Common questions

My town collapsed and I do not know why. Open the ledger and look at Money and Chamber. Towns usually fail one of three ways: the treasury runs dry, the chamber withdraws its confidence at an election, or, under an absolute crown, standing sits under the court's patience long enough that you are removed. Each one is visible before it happens.

Buildings stopped working. From the Colonial age plots need water, and from the Industrial age they need the grid. A building outside a water or power radius switches off, and an off building still costs upkeep while earning nothing. The Power and Water overlays show coverage.

Where are my saves? On your device, in the app's own storage. They are never uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them. The ledger's export option produces a text copy you can keep yourself.

Does it need the internet? No. Sovereign makes no network requests at all. It works on a plane, in a tunnel, anywhere.

Are there purchases? No. You pay once. No advertising, no subscriptions, no timers, nothing to unlock with money.
